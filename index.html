Longrun

Sort, filter and download the Meta Ad Library

The Meta Ad Library shows you every ad an advertiser is running. It just will not let you sort them, filter them, or get them out.

Longrun fixes that. Try it free for 7 days, then $19/month or $119/year.

Add to Chrome

What it does

  • Loads the whole catalogue. Auto-scroll pages through the results until there is nothing left, so you are working with all of an advertiser's ads rather than the first handful.
  • Sorts by how long an ad has run. The Ad Library knows every start date. It just will not sort by it.
  • Finds the creatives being scaled. Meta reports how many ad sets share each creative. One means they are testing it; several means they are backing it with budget.
  • Shows where every ad points, with ad-network redirects labelled as redirects rather than passed off as the destination.
  • Exports to CSV — nineteen columns, with long ad IDs intact instead of mangled into scientific notation.
  • Downloads creatives in bulk, foldered by advertiser, throttled and cancellable.

What it does not do

Longrun does not put a spend figure on every ad. Meta does not publish spend for commercial ads — it publishes EU and UK reach, one ad at a time. Any spend number is reach multiplied by two guesses, frequency and CPM, and lands in a range about five times wide.

Reach is available on request instead: one ad per second, on your own Facebook session, with a counter and a stop button. Slow on purpose. Firing hundreds of requests at once is how people get their account rate-limited, and it is not our account to spend.

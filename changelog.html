Changelog

Every release, dated.

Meta changes the Ad Library without announcing it. When that happens a column quietly goes blank, and the only thing that fixes it is somebody still being here. This page is the record of that.

0.2.1 24 September 2026
  • Reliability checks against the latest Facebook changes.
  • Renamed from AdLib to Longrun. Same tool, same subscription, clearer name.
0.2.0 16 September 2026
  • Subscriptions, with a 7-day free trial that needs no account and no email.
  • Licence keys, so one subscription works on every computer you use.
0.1.0 7 September 2026
  • First release. Sort the Ad Library by how long an ad has been running.
  • Find the creatives an advertiser is scaling, not just testing.
  • Filter by landing domain, search everything loaded, export 19 columns to CSV.
  • Bulk-download every creative, foldered by advertiser.
  • Look up EU and UK reach, one ad at a time, with a stop button.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

AdLib is a Chrome extension that reorganises data the Meta Ad Library has already loaded in your browser. It has no server, no accounts and no analytics.

What AdLib collects

Nothing. No personal information, no browsing history, no account details, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports.

What AdLib stores

One thing, on your own computer, in Chrome's local extension storage:

  • Your interface preferences — the sort order you picked, which filters are switched on, and whether the panel is collapsed.

That is the complete list. No ad data is stored. Nothing persists after you close the tab except those preferences, and removing the extension erases them.

What AdLib transmits

Nothing, to us or to anyone else. AdLib has no server and sends no data anywhere.

Almost everything it does is pure reading: it reorganises the ad data facebook.com has already sent to your browser as you scroll.

There is one exception, and it happens only when you press a button. Meta publishes EU and UK reach figures one ad at a time. If you ask AdLib to look those up, it asks facebook.com for them — one ad at a time, one second apart, using your existing Facebook session, exactly as though you had opened each ad's transparency panel yourself. A count is shown as it runs and you can stop it at any point.

Those replies are read in your browser and shown to you. They are not stored beyond the tab and are not sent anywhere.

Downloads

When you save ad images or videos, the files transfer directly from Meta's content delivery network to your computer. They do not pass through any server operated by us, because no such server exists.

Where AdLib runs

Only on https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/. It does not run on any other Facebook page, on any other website, or when you are not looking at the Ad Library. It cannot read your Facebook account, profile, messages, friends, or any page other than the public Ad Library you have open.

Third parties

None. AdLib includes no analytics service, no advertising SDK, no error reporting service, and no third-party code that phones home.

Your data rights

There is no data to request, correct, export or delete, because none is collected. Your locally stored preferences are removed when you uninstall the extension.

Children

AdLib is a tool for advertising professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.

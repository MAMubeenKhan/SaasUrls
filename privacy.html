Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

AdLib is a Chrome extension that reorganises data the Meta Ad Library has already loaded in your browser. It runs no server of its own, has no analytics and no telemetry.

AdLib is a paid subscription with a free trial. Subscriptions are handled by ExtensionPay, which is the one place any personal information is involved. That is described in full below rather than buried.

What AdLib collects

One thing: your email address, and only if you start a trial or subscribe.

That email is collected by our payment provider, ExtensionPay, so that your subscription can be recognised on any computer you install AdLib on. AdLib reads it back from ExtensionPay to show you which account is active.

Nothing else. No browsing history, no account details, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, and nothing at all about the ads you look at. If you never start a trial, no personal information is involved at any point.

What AdLib stores

One thing, on your own computer, in Chrome's local extension storage:

  • Your interface preferences — the sort order you picked, which filters are switched on, and whether the panel is collapsed.

That is the complete list. No ad data is stored. Nothing persists after you close the tab except those preferences, and removing the extension erases them.

What AdLib transmits

Nothing about you, your browsing, or the ads you look at.

The extension asks ExtensionPay whether your subscription is active. That request carries an installation identifier and nothing else — not the page you are on, not what you searched for, not a single ad.

Everything else it does is pure reading: it reorganises the ad data facebook.com has already sent to your browser as you scroll.

There is one exception, and it happens only when you press a button. Meta publishes EU and UK reach figures one ad at a time. If you ask AdLib to look those up, it asks facebook.com for them — one ad at a time, one second apart, using your existing Facebook session, exactly as though you had opened each ad's transparency panel yourself. A count is shown as it runs and you can stop it at any point.

Those replies are read in your browser and shown to you. They are not stored beyond the tab and are not sent anywhere.

Downloads

When you save ad images or videos, the files transfer directly from Meta's content delivery network to your computer. They do not pass through any server operated by us, because no such server exists.

Where AdLib runs

Only on https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/. It does not run on any other Facebook page, on any other website, or when you are not looking at the Ad Library. It cannot read your Facebook account, profile, messages, friends, or any page other than the public Ad Library you have open.

Third parties

One: ExtensionPay, which handles trials and subscriptions, and which in turn uses Stripe to process payments.

Your card details are entered on Stripe’s own pages and are never seen by, passed through, or stored by AdLib. AdLib learns only whether your subscription is active, and the email address you signed up with.

Beyond that: no analytics service, no advertising SDK, no error reporting service, and no other third-party code.

Your data rights

If you have never started a trial, there is no data to request, correct, export or delete, because none was collected.

If you have subscribed, your email address and billing record are held by ExtensionPay. To see, correct, export or delete them, write to mamubeenkhan@gmail.com and the request will be actioned with ExtensionPay, or contact ExtensionPay directly. Deleting your billing record cancels the subscription it belongs to.

Your locally stored preferences are removed when you uninstall the extension.

Children

AdLib is a tool for advertising professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
